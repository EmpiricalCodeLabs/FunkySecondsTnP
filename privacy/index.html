Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how the Funky Seconds mobile app ("the Game") handles information. The Game is developed by Benic van Wyk, trading as EmpiricalCodeLabs, based in the Netherlands ("we," "us," "our"). We are responsible for any personal data we receive, as described in this policy.

In short: the Game works entirely offline. Everything you enter stays on your device, and the Game does not send any information to us or to anyone else.

2. Information Stored on Your Device

2.1 What You Enter

You do not need an account, and we do not ask for personal information. If you choose to, you can enter player and team names and create custom cards. These are stored only on your device.

2.2 What the Game Stores Automatically

To work, the Game stores the following on your device:

  • Team configurations
  • Game settings and preferences, including your chosen languages
  • Custom cards you create
  • Your current game, so you can continue it later
  • Game progress and statistics
  • Card usage history, used to deal fresh cards in future games
  • The date you accepted the Terms and Conditions and this Privacy Policy

2.3 What We Receive

None of this information is sent to us. The Game does not have permission to access the internet, and it contains no analytics, advertising, crash reporting or tracking.

3. Links That Open Other Apps

3.1 Card Search

You can choose to look up a card word online. The Game then passes the word to your device's web browser, which opens a search on an external search engine. When you do this:

  • The search engine receives your search, as with any search you make
  • We do not see, track or store your searches
  • Nothing from the search is returned to the Game or to us
  • The search engine's own privacy policy applies

3.2 Rate & Review

The Rate & Review option opens the Funky Seconds page in the Google Play Store app or in your web browser. Your use of Google Play is covered by Google's privacy policy.

4. How Your Information Is Used

Information stored on your device is used only by the Game, on your device, to:

  • Save your settings and preferences
  • Let you continue a game you have not finished
  • Deal cards you have not seen recently
  • Manage your teams and custom cards

5. Storage and Security

5.1 Local Storage Only

All Game data is kept in the Game's private storage on your device, using Android's standard app storage (such as SharedPreferences). Android keeps this storage separate from other apps. Because the Game never sends this data anywhere:

  • It stays under your control
  • We cannot access it
  • The Game does not share it with anyone

5.2 Device Backups

If backup is turned on in your device settings, Android may include the Game's data in your device backup, for example in your Google account. That backup is managed by Android and your backup provider, not by us, and their privacy policy applies. A backup may restore the Game's data when you reinstall the Game or set up a new device.

5.3 Security

Keeping your data on your device, with no internet access, is the main way the Game protects it. No storage method is completely secure, so we recommend protecting your device with a screen lock.

6. No Sharing or Third-Party Services

We do not sell, share or transfer any information about you. The Game contains no third-party analytics, advertising, crash reporting or tracking services. The Game only hands information to another app when you choose to search for a card word or open the Play Store, as described in section 3.

7. Contacting Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or your data, email us at funkysecondsgame@gmail.com.

If you email us, we receive your email address, your name if you include it, and whatever you write. We use this only to answer you, based on our legitimate interest in responding to your message. We do not add you to a mailing list or share your email with anyone. Emails are stored in our Gmail account, so Google stores them on our behalf, possibly outside the European Economic Area.

We delete emails once your question has been dealt with, and at the latest 12 months after our last message to each other, unless the law requires us to keep them longer.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

Data on your device is kept until you delete it. You can:

  • Reset game settings in the in-game options
  • Delete custom cards and clear the discard pile in Deck Management
  • Delete all Game data by clearing the app's storage in your device settings or by uninstalling the Game (and removing the Game's data from your device backup, if you use one)

Emails you send us are kept as described in section 7. We hold no other data about you.

9. Your Rights

Under data protection law, including the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), you have the right to access, correct or delete personal data we hold about you, to restrict or object to how we use it, and to receive a copy of it. The only personal data we could hold is an email you sent us. To make a request, email funkysecondsgame@gmail.com.

You also have the right to complain to a data protection authority. In the Netherlands this is the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens (https://autoriteitpersoonsgegevens.nl). You can also contact the authority in the country where you live.

10. Children's Privacy

The Game is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because the Game sends no information to us, we hold no information about the people who play it. If you believe a child has emailed us, contact us and we will delete the message. Parents and guardians can supervise their children's device use and remove the Game and its data at any time.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will publish the updated version in the Game and at https://empiricalcodelabs.github.io/FunkySecondsTnP/privacy/ with a new "Last updated" date. If a change significantly affects how we handle your information, we will point it out in the Game.
